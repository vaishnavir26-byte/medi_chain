1 Patient Consent Authority

Patients hold the cryptographic keys to grant or revoke provider access for specific scopes (EHR, Labs, Rx) and expiration windows (1h, 24h, 7d).

2 Decoupled Storage (HIPAA/GDPR)

Medical files are NEVER stored on-chain. Sensitive files live in secure off-chain storage; only mathematical SHA-256 hashes are anchored to blocks.

3 Tamper-Evident Audit Ledger

Every permission change, data read, clinical write, or blocked attempt is permanently sealed into append-only SHA-256 blocks for compliance auditing.
